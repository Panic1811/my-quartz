**Hero balance** is the practice of ensuring each character in a game is _fun to play_, _fair to play against_, and _viable in most contexts_. It’s not just about numbers—it’s about _player experience_, _perception_, and _design clarity_. Competitive games like **Overwatch**, **Valorant**, **League of Legends**, and **Marvel Rivals** use a blend of **data-driven** and **perception-driven** methods to evaluate hero balance.

## 🧪 What Is Hero Balance?

Balance isn't only about achieving a 50% winrate. It’s about:

- Ensuring **multiple heroes are viable** across skill tiers, maps, and metas.
    
- Preventing **unfun, frustrating, or oppressive experiences**.
    
- Maintaining the **identity and playstyle** of each hero.
    
- Supporting **strategic diversity** in team compositions.
    

> “We wanted all our heroes to be fun to play without being unfair to play against.”  
> — _Marvel Rivals Developers_ [1](#user-content-fn-source1)

## 📊 Data-Driven Balancing

Developers collect gameplay data across millions of matches to guide decisions. Key metrics include:

- **Winrate** (general + by rank/map/comp)
    
- **Pick/Ban Rate**
    
- **Mastery Curves** (how winrate changes over time)
    
- **Performance Ratings** (e.g., Marvel Rivals calculates combat performance relative to rank baselines) [2](#user-content-fn-source2)
    

### ⚠️ Nuances & Caveats

Raw data can be misleading without proper context:

- **Skill tier effects**: Some heroes dominate at high ranks but underperform at low ones (e.g., Genji, Widowmaker).
    
- **Map bias**: Some heroes are only viable on specific maps (e.g., Symmetra winrate inflated by being picked only on favorable maps) [3](#user-content-fn-source3).
    
- **Mirrored picks**: When both teams pick the same hero, winrates flatten. “Unmirrored” data is more accurate (e.g., Sojourn had a 50% winrate but was still dominant) [4](#user-content-fn-source4).
    
- **Team synergy**: Some heroes are only strong in certain team comps (e.g., Sombra + Ana nano boosts).
    
- **Last-minute swaps**: Heroes picked when a team is already winning or losing skew stats (e.g., high winrate from being picked only in winning moments).
    
- **Complexity**: Harder heroes have lower winrates initially (e.g., Yasuo started at 36% winrate before players learned him) [5](#user-content-fn-source5).
    

> “Everyone looks at win rates. But we need to go deeper to achieve stronger balancing.”  
> — _Riot Games Dev Blog_ [5](#user-content-fn-source5)

## 😡 Perception-Driven Balancing

Player **sentiment** plays a huge role. Games often track:

- **Survey responses**
    
- **Social media/Reddit/forums**
    
- **In-game reporting systems**
    
- **Frustration spikes** (e.g., sudden increases in player complaints)
    

> “We utilize player perception as flags for potential problems… but perception is not an immediate call to action.”  
> — _Riot’s Maple Nectar_ [6](#user-content-fn-source6)

### 🔍 Why Perception Matters

- **Frustrating heroes** (e.g., burst assassins or crowd control spam) might be numerically fair but _feel_ oppressive.
    
- **Loud feedback** often reveals issues not yet in the data (e.g., Valorant rebalanced supports after support players complained about survivability _before_ stats showed it) [7](#user-content-fn-source7).
    
- **New players’ UX** can be harmed by design confusion, even if stats are fine (e.g., League once discovered new players didn’t know how to position their fingers on QWER) [5](#user-content-fn-source5).
    

### ⚠️ Risks of Overreacting

- **Misleading sentiment**: A hero perceived as “overpowered” might be statistically average (e.g., Genji and Soldier 76 once had nearly identical stats despite very different player opinions) [8](#user-content-fn-source8).
    
- **Rank biases**: What feels strong in Bronze may not matter in Diamond.
    
- **Knee-jerk nerfs** can kill a hero’s identity (e.g., Zed’s damage feels unfair to casuals but is integral to his role as an assassin) [6](#user-content-fn-source6).

## 🔁 Reworks: When Balance Isn’t Enough

Sometimes, a hero’s **kit or design** is fundamentally flawed, and no amount of tweaking numbers will fix them. In these cases, full **reworks** are needed.

- **Examples:**
    
    - _Overwatch’s Bastion_: Removed self-repair, reworked into a more mobile artillery character.
        
    - _League’s Aatrox_: Completely changed his identity from a sustain-based duelist to a skillshot-heavy juggernaut.
        
    - _Marvel Rivals devs_ noted that if a hero consistently underperforms **across all skill levels**, it’s usually a **design issue**, not a balance issue [2](#user-content-fn-source2).
        

> “Sometimes, we just missed the mark. If no amount of tuning works, we go back to the drawing board.”  
> — _Riot Games Dev Blog_ [5](#user-content-fn-source5)

## ⚖️ Data vs. Perception: Which Is Better?

|**Data-Driven**|**Perception-Driven**|
|---|---|
|Objective and scalable|Captures human experience|
|Helps detect meta trends|Detects frustration early|
|Can miss emotional impact|Can be noisy or biased|
|Requires interpretation|Requires caution to avoid overreaction|

### 🧩 Best Practice: Hybrid Approach

Most developers aim to be **data-informed, not data-driven**:

- Combine stats with community feedback.
    
- Use sentiment as a flag, then verify with analytics.
    
- Consider hero impact **across all levels of play**, not just one tier.
    
- Accept that **perfect 50/50 balance is not the goal**—fun, counterplay, and variety are more important.
    

> “We won’t seek perfect 50/50 balance. That would make the game robotic and formulaic.”  
> — _Valorant Dev Blog_ [9](#user-content-fn-source9)

## ✅ Summary

- **Hero balance** is a mix of **fun**, **fairness**, and **viability**.
    
- **Data** helps spot trends, but must be interpreted carefully.
    
- **Perception** identifies pain points, but is often subjective.
    
- **Reworks** are necessary when design, not numbers, causes imbalance.
    
- **The healthiest games balance both approaches**—statistical rigor + emotional insight.

## Data-Driven Balancing

- **Objective Metrics:** Developers mine gameplay data for signs of imbalance. Common metrics include _win rate_, _pick/ban rate_, _match outcomes_, and _engagement data_. For example, Valorant’s balance team tracks agent winrate by rank/map, pick rate, and how many games it takes to hit an agent’s “true” winrate (mastery curve)[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/trust-the-balance-process-data-and-insights/#:~:text=,average%20round%20outcome%20by%20map). League of Legends analytics “convert billions of hours of gameplay into information”[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=As%20Rudi%20Bonaparte%2C%20who%20focuses,to%20carry%20him%20towards%20balance). Marvel Rivals computes a “hero performance rating” each match from combat stats vs. rank baselines[marvelrivals.com](https://www.marvelrivals.com/devdiaries/20250426/40954_1230856.html#:~:text=In%20each%20match%2C%20hero%20performance,according%20to%20their%20usage%20time). These large-scale stats can reveal when a hero is over- or under-powered across the player base.
    
- **Nuances in Interpretation:** Raw stats can be misleading without context. Skill tier plays a huge role: a hero may have ~50% winrate overall but be _much_ higher or lower at top ranks. Riot’s Yasuo example shows a very low initial winrate (36%) that climbed as players learned him[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=rate,to%20carry%20him%20towards%20balance). Overwatch’s devs discovered _mirrored picks_ obscure imbalance – if both teams pick Sojourn, her winrate stays near 50% despite being overpowered[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=One%20weakness%20of%20this%20metric,has%20to%20lose%2C%20after%20all). They thus use “unmirrored” analysis to see her true edge[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=One%20weakness%20of%20this%20metric,has%20to%20lose%2C%20after%20all). Similarly, **situational picks** skew stats: Symmetra’s win rate is high because players only pick her on favorable objectives and swap off if losing[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=Take%20Symmetra%2C%20for%20example,rate%20in%20a%20positive%20direction).
    
- **Team/Map Synergy:** Hero effectiveness often depends on map and team composition. A Sentinel-heavy meta in Valorant might favor defenders, while Duelist picks favor attackers[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/trust-the-balance-process-data-and-insights/#:~:text=For%20example%2C%20we%20don%E2%80%99t%20know,the%20state%20of%20the%20game). Overwatch teams track hero performance by map and map-type, factoring in switches[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=Fear%20not%2C%20that%20scenario%20is,%E2%80%9D). Unintended synergies can also emerge (e.g. Soldier 76 + Sombra “nano-boost” exploits) that data will highlight after play, prompting targeted tweaks[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=being%20explored,affected%20in%20the%20May%205).
    
- **Pitfalls:** Over-reliance on raw data can backfire. Small sample sizes (rare heroes) can show spurious winrates[invenglobal.com](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10127/overwatch-dev-frustrates-owl-pros-and-high-elo-players-but-provides-helpful-clarity-on-game-balancing-perspective#:~:text=In%20his%20statement%2C%20Josh%20claimed,already%20going%20to%20win%20anyway). For instance, an Overwatch dev noted that Genji and Soldier:76 had nearly identical win/pick stats despite community claims about their power[invenglobal.com](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10127/overwatch-dev-frustrates-owl-pros-and-high-elo-players-but-provides-helpful-clarity-on-game-balancing-perspective#:~:text=Josh%20returned%20to%20clarify%20his,complaining%20about%2C%20specifically%20Baptiste%20and). An inflated winrate in low pickup heroes might come from one-tricks or “last-minute switches” in winning games[invenglobal.com](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10127/overwatch-dev-frustrates-owl-pros-and-high-elo-players-but-provides-helpful-clarity-on-game-balancing-perspective#:~:text=In%20his%20statement%2C%20Josh%20claimed,already%20going%20to%20win%20anyway). Developers also warn that perfectly 50/50 stats for every hero would make for a robotic game – they prefer some decision-space even if stats aren’t exactly balanced[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,making%20decisions%20provides%20decision%20points).
    

> _“If nobody is bothering to use a certain skill, we’ll revisit the champion… so that… all of their abilities [are] useful.”_[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=If%20we%20want%20to%20understand,all%20of%20their%20abilities%20useful) Riot emphasizes using analytics to spot underused strengths or weaknesses, but also notes that even thorough data must be “contextualized” with human insight[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,won%E2%80%99t%20be%20entirely%20data%20driven)[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=Ultimately%2C%20research%20at%20Riot%20is,informed).

## Perception-Driven Balancing

- **Player Sentiment Signals:** Community feedback (forums, social media, surveys) can highlight perceived issues that raw data might miss. Phrases like “frustrating to play against” or “that hero feels unfun” are often flagged. Riot uses lab tests and surveys to catch problems “impossible to measure with numbers”[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=BEING%20DATA,LEARN%20AS%20MUCH%20AS%20POSSIBLE). Valorant explicitly sends player surveys asking what’s fun or frustrating to supplement game data[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,represented%20in%20the%20game%20data). This _sentiment data_ helps interpret stats and find “experience issues that aren’t represented in the game data”[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,represented%20in%20the%20game%20data).
    
- **Global Perspective:** Perception varies by player level, region, or mode. Riot’s designers note that what’s annoying in one region or rank might not be in another[nexus.leagueoflegends.com](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2018/02/ask-riot-balancing-champions/#:~:text=Another%20thing%20we%20consider%20is,design%20with%20a%20global%20perspective). A champion may “feel oppressive” to casual players but be manageable for pros. Thus, perception triggers investigation but doesn’t always dictate change. As Riot’s Live Gameplay lead summarizes:
    
    > _“We utilize player perception as flags for potential problems… [but perception is] not an immediate call to action that something needs to be done.”_[nexus.leagueoflegends.com](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2018/02/ask-riot-balancing-champions/#:~:text=To%20summarize%E2%80%94we%20utilize%20player%20perception,something%20needs%20to%20be%20done)
    
- **Pitfalls of Feelings:** Relying solely on gut can cause knee-jerk nerfs or ignore long-term data. A common example in League is the assassin **Zed**: players “dying very quickly” to him feels unfair, but Zed’s role is to burst targets. Developers argue nerfing his combo would break his identity; instead, they ensure adequate counters (armor, abilities) exist[nexus.leagueoflegends.com](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2018/02/ask-riot-balancing-champions/#:~:text=All%20of%20the%20above%20impact,just%20be%20to%20nerf%20him). Community misconceptions can mislead devs: an Overwatch designer pointed out that one of the “perceived strongest” heroes and one “perceived weakest” hero had nearly _identical_ pick and win rates[invenglobal.com](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10127/overwatch-dev-frustrates-owl-pros-and-high-elo-players-but-provides-helpful-clarity-on-game-balancing-perspective#:~:text=Josh%20returned%20to%20clarify%20his,complaining%20about%2C%20specifically%20Baptiste%20and), showing that loud voices don’t always represent the true state.
    

> _“Players have a lot of knowledge about the game and what feels strong or weak, [but] they don’t have as much data or experience to know what decisions devs need to make.”_[invenglobal.com](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10127/overwatch-dev-frustrates-owl-pros-and-high-elo-players-but-provides-helpful-clarity-on-game-balancing-perspective#:~:text=The%20final%20point%20is%20that,than%20current%20GM%20players%20give) Overwatch’s Josh Noh (game director) echoes that players may sense problems, but developers see the full dataset and must consider all contexts – including levels of play and pick/ban trends – before adjusting heroes.

## Balancing the Two Approaches

- **Data-Driven Pros:** Large-scale, objective, and repeatable. It surfaces issues across millions of games and prevents wild guesswork. For instance, league devs use winrate bands plus ban rates to set clear targets for balance[nexus.leagueoflegends.com](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2019/05/dev-champion-balance-framework/#:~:text=Win%20rate%20tells%20us%20most,put%20on%20their%20win%20rate), and Valorant’s formal process monitors many metrics (round winrates, economy, etc.) to spot imbalances[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24197405/step-into-the-stadium-reddit-ama/#:~:text=In%20terms%20of%20just%20raw,what%20we%E2%80%99re%20considering%2C%20we%E2%80%99ve%20got).
    
- **Data-Driven Cons:** Data lacks emotional impact – it won’t tell you if a mechanic feels _annoying_. It can miss emerging strategies (until used enough to show in stats). Overwatch found that pure winrate still needed human context: their charts highlight that Symmetra’s winrate looked high because of play patterns[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=This%20chart%20illustrates%20the%20unmirrored,in%20which%20they%20are%20generated).
    
- **Perception-Driven Pros:** Captures player sentiment and emergent trends immediately. Frustration signals can preempt data (e.g. many support players reported low survivability in OW beta, triggering changes before winrates spiked)[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=balance,the%20%200%20May%205). Developers’ research labs also uncover UX issues (like new players not knowing where to put their hands on the keyboard[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=Labs%20let%20us%20catch%20things,they%20ignore%20the%20keyboard%20altogether)).
    
- **Perception-Driven Cons:** Highly subjective and noisy. Feedback can be polarized or based on limited play. Developers warn that balancing for the squeaky wheel can backfire. Riot’s framework seeks to minimize **subjectivity**, noting that inconsistent handling of perception is “more frustrating” than individual balance issues[nexus.leagueoflegends.com](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2019/05/dev-champion-balance-framework/#:~:text=The%20main%20improvement%20we%E2%80%99d%20like,of%20the%20past%20or%20future).
    

> _“We won’t be entirely data driven. We won’t seek a perfect 50/50 balance.”_[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,making%20decisions%20provides%20decision%20points) Both Riot (Valorant) and Blizzard (Overwatch) stress that good balance isn’t purely numeric. Valorant’s lead designer explicitly says they will “rely on human judgement to contextualize game data” and accept that perfect 50/50 winrates would make decisions “robotic and formulaic”[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,making%20decisions%20provides%20decision%20points). Blizzard similarly notes that balancing is “purposeful” and uses _many sources, data-driven or otherwise_[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=balance,the%20%200%20May%205).

## Toward a Healthy Balance

Most developers advocate a **hybrid, data-informed approach**. They use analytics as a diagnostic tool but combine it with player feedback and expert judgment. Valorant’s balance team declares data “won’t be the _be-all and end-all_… [but] one of the many tools we can use”[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/trust-the-balance-process-data-and-insights/#:~:text=For%20example%2C%20we%20don%E2%80%99t%20know,the%20state%20of%20the%20game). They continuously refine their understanding by correlating telemetry with direct player input[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/trust-the-balance-process-data-and-insights/#:~:text=That%20being%20said%2C%20we%E2%80%99re%20constantly,and%20how%20we%20can%20adjust). Riot’s philosophy is to “find that balance around data and gut reactions so we can be data-informed”[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=Ultimately%2C%20research%20at%20Riot%20is,informed).

Key takeaways:

- **Balance metrics** should be interpreted in context (skill level, maps, team comps, playtime). Win rate alone can be deceptive without adjusting for these factors[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=One%20weakness%20of%20this%20metric,has%20to%20lose%2C%20after%20all)[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23787377/overwatch-2-pvp-beta-analysis-how-data-and-community-feedback-inform-game-balance/#:~:text=Take%20Symmetra%2C%20for%20example,rate%20in%20a%20positive%20direction).
    
- **Perception matters** as an early warning: if many players find a hero “unfair” or “unfun,” developers investigate the causes. But they won’t reflexively nerf without evidence (as Riot’s Maple Nectar emphasizes)[nexus.leagueoflegends.com](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2018/02/ask-riot-balancing-champions/#:~:text=To%20summarize%E2%80%94we%20utilize%20player%20perception,something%20needs%20to%20be%20done).
    
- **Iterative tuning** is crucial. Heroes may shift power after a few patches as meta evolves. Developers aim to keep the game “new, fresh, and changing” at the right pace[overwatch.blizzard.com](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24197405/step-into-the-stadium-reddit-ama/#:~:text=Even%20the%20most%20beloved%20%E2%80%9Cmetas%E2%80%9D,given%20state%20of%20the%20game).
    
- **Player satisfaction vs. integrity:** Designers often have to balance making players happy with making the game healthy. As Valorant’s team notes, they “won’t make every player happy” if it hurts the overall game[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,won%E2%80%99t%20be%20entirely%20data%20driven).
    

In summary, **data-driven balancing** provides the numbers and breadth to spot and measure balance issues; **perception-driven balancing** provides the human perspective and catches what stats miss. The healthiest games emerge when developers **blend both**: using data and surveys to pinpoint problems, then applying design judgement to adjust heroes for fairness and fun[playvalorant.com](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/how-we-balance-valorant/#:~:text=,making%20decisions%20provides%20decision%20points)[riotgames.com](https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/disciplines/insights/get-data-informed-on-the-insights-blog#:~:text=Ultimately%2C%20research%20at%20Riot%20is,informed). This hybrid strategy helps ensure a meta where varied heroes can thrive and players remain engaged.
