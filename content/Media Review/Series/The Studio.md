# My Thoughts

Interesting premise, from the studios perspective in Hollywood. Well shot, long sequences, felt quite immersive to watch. Some episodes felt a little silly, but was still a good time. A satire take on Hollywood *from* people already in Hollywood might be a little biased, but for people who enjoy Showbizz it was fun seeing the various cameos and a little sneak peak into the world.
