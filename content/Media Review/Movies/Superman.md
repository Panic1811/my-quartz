## 📺 My Thoughts

Started of as fun, but ended up just being alright. Came across as a very weak superman (where perception was that he was overpowered - ironic). Quite a light movie, I did like Lex's move sequence, however, Superman did not win a single fight without assistance. No "Badass" moments and some scenes felt like video game cut scenes. The coolest action sequence wasn't even superman's but Mr Terrifics! Score was quite mute, paling in comparison To MoS Zimmer and solely leveraging John William's iconic score. A little too much childish humor for my taste and most of the plot sequences seemed irrelevant. Too much emphasis on the dog and tying in geo political events for empathy points? Wish it was just a well crafted movie instead. 

Mirza was seeing it for the 2nd time, and he thought it was alright, still paling in comparison to his MoS review. Acting was alright, can get behind it, just lack of "Goosbump" esque moments and a little too light.
