## 📺 My Thoughts

Was a fun movie but expected more. As expected, the racing scenes were beautifully shot however the plot felt limited and in my opinion the score was a little too quiet. Perhaps it was the cinema but given how loud F1 actually is and how Zimmer is infamous for diluting dialogue with louds scores (perhaps maybe just a Nolan thing), I thought it would be a bit louder. Otherwise an exciting watch and one of those movies which reminds you why visiting a cinema provides authentic experiences.
