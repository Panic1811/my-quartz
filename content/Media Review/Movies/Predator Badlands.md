## 📺 My Thoughts

Unexpected movie, went in thinking I'm about to watch Alien esque horror movie, to my surprise, a sci-fi one instead. 

Was pretty cool visually, wished we saw it in IMAX instead, however, wasn't as engaging throughout, lots of sequences felt inconsequential and not as exciting (man vs wild?) Saw parts of Avatar in there with the Pandora setting. Other than that it was alright, decent watch.

Mirza quite enjoyed it and Mariam thought it was quite boring.
