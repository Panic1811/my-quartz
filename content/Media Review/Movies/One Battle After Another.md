## 📺 My Thoughts

Sadly another movie with incredibly good ratings which didn't translate for me in reality. While it was incredibly well acted, the plot seemed a bit unfinished or unclear even with its 3 hour runtime. Didn't really feel too connected with the characters goals and aspirates, so the stakes felt even more flat to a degree. Great cinematography and score and phenomenal acting, just wish the plot was a little bit less abstract and more of a clear linear exciting story.
