> [!NOTE] Context
> The idea originates from Graham Rawlinson's 1976 PhD thesis at Nottingham University, which explored how scrambling middle letters affects reading. It works best for short words with context, adjacent swaps, and preserved sounds, but reading slows by about 11% and fails for complex jumbles. The meme viralized around 2003.
