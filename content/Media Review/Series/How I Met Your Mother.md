# My Thoughts

Watched it quite late, 2024 I believe. Show was great, felt like each episode (20 mins) was a condensed movie in terms of plot they managed to fit in. I remember thinking every episode felt like a movie, not in duration, but in terms of plot depth. So for that, I must give it props on great writing. While the ending was controversial and I would have also preferred the alternative ending (BTS scenes) it's still good fun.
